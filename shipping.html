Shipping

Where we ship

We currently ship to US addresses only. International shipping is coming soon.

Shipping cost

Shipping to US addresses is free.

Where orders ship from

Our products are shipped directly from our suppliers' warehouses. Most items ship from China, and a few ship from US warehouses. If you order several items, they may arrive in separate packages.

Delivery time

Most orders arrive in about 7 to 14 business days, including 1 to 3 business days of processing. Items that ship from a US warehouse usually arrive sooner, in about 3 to 8 business days. Each product page shows its own estimate. Delivery times are estimates and can be longer during holidays or carrier delays.

Order questions

For the status of an order, email lalaayenglobalplaza@gmail.com with your order number.

Damaged, wrong or missing items

See Returns & Refunds. Returns are free within 14 days of delivery.
