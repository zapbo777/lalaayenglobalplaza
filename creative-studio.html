FAQ & Terms

Is a deposit required?

A 30% deposit may be required after your project scope and quote are confirmed. Work begins after the deposit and all required materials are received.

What is the refund policy?

Cancellation and refund terms are confirmed in the project quote. Deposits may be non-refundable after customized work begins. If Lala Ayen LLC cannot deliver the agreed service, the customer will receive an appropriate refund for the undelivered portion.

May I submit photos, music, logos, or artwork?

Yes, but you must own them or have permission to use them commercially. You are responsible for permissions for materials you provide.

Do you use AI-assisted tools?

AI-assisted tools may be used in production for visuals, music, voice, or editing. Final deliverables and commercial-use rights depend on the tools, assets, and license terms used for the project.

What will I receive?

Deliverables may include MP4 video files, image files, captions, thumbnails, or social-media copy, depending on the selected package. Final formats are confirmed in the quote.

What counts as a revision?

An included revision means reasonable changes to the agreed concept. A new concept, new script, or substantial change in direction may require an additional fee.
