Returns & Refunds

Free returns within 14 days

You can return any item, including lingerie, within 14 days of delivery, for any reason. Returns are free: we send you a prepaid return label.

How to return an item

  1. Email lalaayenglobalplaza@gmail.com with your order number to start a return. Tell us which item(s) you are returning and why. (The chat on our shop pages can also walk you through these steps.)
  2. We email you a free prepaid return label.
  3. Pack the item, attach the label and ship it to the address on the label. That address may be a supplier or partner warehouse rather than our office, so please always use the label we send.

Damaged or wrong item

If an item arrives damaged, or you received the wrong item, email us photos of the item and the package. We will send a replacement or a refund, whichever you prefer. The photos help us file a claim with our supplier.

Wrong size

If something does not fit, request a free return as above and place a new order for the size you need, or email us and we will help you choose.

Refunds

Refunds go back to your original payment method once your return is processed.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order before it ships. Email us as soon as possible with your order number. Once an order has shipped, use the free return process above.

Affiliate items

Items bought through affiliate links on other websites follow that merchant's own return policy.
