Privacy Notice

LALA AYEN GLOBAL PLAZA offers products and creative services. Emails you choose to send to us may include your name, contact details and inquiry. The public site's static inquiry guide prepares an email draft on your own device and does not send it automatically.

Customers can create an account with their name, email address and a password. Passwords are never stored as written; we keep only a salted hash of them. After you sign in, a secure sign-in cookie keeps you logged in for up to 30 days across the whole site. Account information is stored with Cloudflare in a Cloudflare D1 database. Sign-in attempts are counted for a short time to stop abuse. Customer reviews, once available, are visible to other visitors with their star rating. Do not post private personal information in reviews. US card payments are processed by Square on its secure checkout page; card details never touch our site. Square shares the order details, your email and your shipping address with us so we can fulfill your order, and we keep an order record in our Cloudflare database. International checkout through Zonos is planned. We do not request card numbers by email.

Your cart and your currency and language choices are saved in your own browser (local storage), not on our servers, until you check out. If you choose a language other than English, the page is translated by Google Translate, which receives the page text and sets a language cookie. Currency conversions use exchange rates that our server fetches from a public rates service (open.er-api.com); no personal information is sent with that request.

Messages you type in the shop chat are processed by Cloudflare Workers AI to answer your questions. We do not sell your data or use chat messages to sell data. Please do not share card numbers or passwords in the chat.

For privacy questions, requests to correct/remove a review, or to delete your account or reset your password, contact lalaayenglobalplaza@gmail.com.

This notice should be reviewed with legal advice before accepting orders.
