Privacy Notice

LALA AYEN GLOBAL PLAZA offers products and creative services. Emails you choose to send to us may include your name, contact details and inquiry. The public site's static inquiry guide prepares an email draft on your own device and does not send it automatically.

Customers can create an account with their name, email address and a password. Passwords are never stored as written; we keep only a salted hash of them. After you sign in, a secure sign-in cookie keeps you logged in for up to 30 days across the whole site. Account information is stored with Cloudflare in a Cloudflare D1 database. Sign-in attempts are counted for a short time to stop abuse. Customer reviews you submit are visible to other visitors with their star rating. Do not post private personal information in reviews. Payments, when enabled, are processed by Zonos, and card details never touch our site. We do not request card numbers by email.

For privacy questions, requests to correct/remove a review, or to delete your account or reset your password, contact lalaayenglobalplaza@gmail.com.

This notice should be reviewed with legal advice before accepting orders.
